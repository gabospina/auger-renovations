These are derived from empirical time-motion studies published in industry standard cost guides (such as RSMeans, APCHQ guides, and Means Estimating) combined with the typical workflow of an RBQ-licensed carpenter or tradesperson. A 6-hour allocation for removing a shower is broken down into specific micro-tasks rather than treated as a single action: ### Detailed Breakdown of the 6-Hour Shower Demolition 1. **Site Protection & Staging (0.75 hr / 45 min)** * Floor runner installation through hallways/stairs. * Polyethylene dust containment zippers over doors and HVAC registers. * Setting up the HEPA air scrubber/shop-vac. 2. **Mechanical Disconnection & Isolation (0.75 hr / 45 min)** * Water line shut-off and isolation caps (crimping PEX or soldering copper end caps). * Removing trim, mixing valve cartridges, and shower head arms. * Cutting or capping the drain tailpiece without cracking the P-trap below the subfloor. 3. **Demolition of Enclosure & Substrate (2.5 hrs)** * Removing tempered glass doors, tracks, and sealant beads (0.75 hr). * Breaking out wall tiles and removing underlying drywall/cement board down to the bare studs (1.25 hrs). * Chiseling out the acrylic base or mud bed/mortar pan without damaging surrounding framing (0.5 hr). 4. **Debris Bagging & Disposal (1.25 hrs)** * Hauling heavy tile shards, wet drywall, and framing scrap out to the trailer/dumpster. * A typical 36"x48" tile shower generates 300 to 500 lbs of heavy rubble requiring multiple trips. 5. **Rough Framing Cleanup & De-Nailing (0.75 hr / 45 min)** * Pulling remaining drywall screws, staple rings, and nails out of the studs. * Vacuuming inside wall cavities to inspect for hidden mold, rotted studs, or leaks. **Total: 6.0 Man-Hours** --- ### Standard Estimation Formulas by Trade The automatic rules in your script follow standard contractor metrics: * **Tile Removal:** $0.15\text{ to }0.25\text{ hours per sq ft}$ (approx. 4 to 6 hours for an 80–100 sq ft shower enclosure). * **Tile Installation & Grouting:** $0.25\text{ to }0.35\text{ hours per sq ft}$ plus layout/cuts. * **Drywall & Joint Finishing:** * Hanging sheets: $0.25\text{ hours per 4'x8' sheet}$ (15 min/sheet). * 3-coat taping & sanding: $0.12\text{ to }0.15\text{ hours per sq ft}$ of surface area. * **Standard Fixtures:** * Toilet replacement: $1.5\text{ to }2.0\text{ hours}$ (pull old wax ring, verify flange, assemble, seal, leak test). * Pre-built vanity (30"-48"): $3.5\text{ to }4.5\text{ hours}$ (leveling, scribing, faucet mount, P-trap & drain hookup, supply lines, silicone bead). --- ### Customizing the Hours
